Privacy.

Your inquiry

The contact form asks for your name, email address, reason for contacting J!NX and an explanation. For an Objects inquiry, you can also identify the category that interests you.

This information is used to receive, review and respond to your inquiry. It does not subscribe you to a newsletter or marketing list.

Email delivery

Submitted inquiries are sent to J!NX by email through Brevo, the email delivery provider. The artist can reply using the email address you supply. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your message.

External platforms

Social and music links open services such as Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Music, Spotify and Apple Music. Those services apply their own privacy terms. A Spotify player is loaded only when you choose to play a release here.

Questions or requests

For questions about information you submitted, including a request to delete an inquiry from the artist’s inbox, contact vandalenzan102@gmail.com.
